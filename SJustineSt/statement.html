Under Articles 21 and 22 of the Illinois Property Tax Code, if a homeowner in Cook County becomes 13 months delinquent in their property tax payments, the county may auction this debt to a private buyer. To “redeem” their debt, the homeowner must repay the buyer for the delinquent taxes plus interest, fines, and fees. If this debt goes unpaid for 30 months, the buyer is entitled to petition the county for a tax deed to the property. Upon transfer of title, the original homeowner is left uncompensated for the difference between their tax debt and the total value of the home.

Researchers at the University of Chicago have shown that as recently as 2025, the county’s property tax system systematically overtaxed Black homeowners. Analysis of historic tax sale and deed data indicates that Black homeowners in Cook County also disproportionately lose their homes through forced tax deed transfers. The Pacific Legal Foundation estimates the total value of stolen equity transferred to tax buyers in Cook County between 2014 and 2021 is $190 million. As of July 2025, Illinois was the only state to sanction irreversible home equity theft for tax debts.

In December of 2022, Cook County homeowners and two nonprofit plaintiffs, Southwest Organizing Project (SWOP) and Palenque LSNA (PLSNA), filed Bell v. Pappas, a class action lawsuit against the county and its treasurer, Maria Pappas. In December 2025, the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois ruled in this case that Cook County’s tax deed policy constituted unfair “takings” and violated the Fifth and Eighth Amendments of the Constitution.

These identical artworks reproduce the Cook County Assessor Image of a property on S Justine St. in Chicago, Cook County, Illinois.

In 2016, the owner of this home was over 13 months delinquent for $203.86 of property taxes. This debt was sold by Cook County at auction. After paying additional administrative fees, the buyer’s total investment in the property equalled $1,968.72. The original owner was unable to redeem this debt, so a tax deed petition was granted to the buyer. Title to the property on S Justine St. was transferred to White Home Properties LLC in February of 2020. Four months later, the home was listed for sale. It sold in January 2021 for $97,500.00.

The disparate prices of these two works reflect these disparate valuations of the pictured home when its title was transferred through the 2020 tax deed petition and the 2021 warranty deed following its arms-length sale. Proceeds from the sale of these artworks will be donated to SWOP and PLSNA, the non-profit litigants in Bell v. Pappas, the ongoing class-action lawsuit against Cook County's tax sale practices.
